VLC Glass 0.2.0 is an independent LibVLC-powered Android media player for 64-bit Android 9 or newer devices.

- Switch between Apple clear and LastWave depth in Settings → Visual & appearance → Liquid glass → Emulate mode.
- Adjust glass depth, prismatic edges, frosting and highlights. Includes looping Anatomy artwork, reduced motion and responsive layouts.
- Includes local media and folder import, saved positions, playlists, queue management, speed controls, subtitles, equalizer, bookmarks, A–B looping, sleep timer, cast selection and picture in picture.
